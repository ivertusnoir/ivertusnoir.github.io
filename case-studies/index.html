Case studies: brief, pipeline, output.

Three projects broken down by premise, process and tools.

Montale Onyx

  • Speculative · Packaging & motion
  • Design, compositing & sound
  • After Effects, Illustrator, Ableton, Blender
  • 2026
Brief
Speculative limited-edition outer-sleeve packaging and teaser for Montale Paris.
Premise
Restraint, surface contrast, and sound carry luxury authority without bottle visuals.
Execution
2D packaging graphics developed in Illustrator, staged under low-key Blender lighting, and assembled in After Effects through precision compositing, expression-driven telemetry, and rhythm-led editing. Original sound design and material-driven foley composed in Ableton Live at 120 BPM.

The Young Ones: Vote

  • Brief-based · Out-of-home motion
  • Design, motion & compositing
  • After Effects, ComfyUI
  • 2026
Brief
Out-of-home motion piece for The Young Ones “Get Out the Vote” campaign.
Premise
Voting is pragmatic, not a search for a perfect candidate.
Execution
A HUD-style interface where animated rings convert civic alignment into a live, calculated metric. Ring behavior is driven by After Effects expressions; supporting imagery is generated in ComfyUI.
Delivery
1080×1920 60fps vertical DOOH sequence, staged in an urban placement mockup.
