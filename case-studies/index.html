Case studies: brief, pipeline, output.

Four projects broken down by premise, process and tools.

Montale Onyx

  • Speculative · Packaging & motion
  • Design, compositing & sound
  • After Effects, Illustrator, Ableton, Blender
  • 2026
Brief
Speculative limited-edition outer-sleeve packaging and teaser for Montale Paris.
Premise
Restraint, surface contrast, and sound carry luxury authority without bottle visuals.
Execution
2D packaging graphics built in Illustrator and staged under low-key Blender lighting. Assembled end-to-end in After Effects with expression-driven telemetry and scored at 120 BPM in Ableton Live.

The Young Ones: Vote

  • Brief-based · Out-of-home motion
  • Design, motion & compositing
  • After Effects, ComfyUI
  • 2026
Brief
Out-of-home motion piece for The Young Ones “Get Out the Vote” campaign.
Premise
Voting is pragmatic, not a search for a perfect candidate.
Execution
A HUD-style interface where animated rings convert civic alignment into a live, calculated metric. Ring behavior is driven by After Effects expressions; supporting imagery is generated in ComfyUI.
Delivery
Vertical out-of-home assets, shown in a placement mockup.

Rammstein — Sonne

  • Self-initiated · Cover & performance visual
  • Audio, cinematography & motion
  • 2026
Brief
Complete audio cover of Rammstein’s “Sonne” with an integrated performance visual.
Audio
Dual-tracked, down-tuned guitars, with picture cut to the track using phase-aligned sync.
Look
Stark cinematography and cyan-red grading. Kinetic typography deployed as glitch-mapped German lyric callouts locked to the percussive hits.
