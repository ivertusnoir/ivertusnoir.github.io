KINETIC OOH CAMPAIGN

RAMMSTEIN — SONNE —
PERFORMANCE VISUAL

01 / 03
  • Self-initiated · Cover & performance visual
  • Audio, cinematography & motion
  • 2026
  • FULL VERSION

A complete audio cover of Rammstein’s “Sonne” with an integrated performance visual. Engineered with dual-tracked, down-tuned guitars and cut to the track with phase-aligned sync. Stark cinematography and cyan-red grading anchor the look, with kinetic typography deployed as glitch-mapped German lyric callouts locked to the track’s percussive hits.
